=== QUICK START ===

1. Connect WeAct 2.9" display to nRF52840-DK
   (See QUICK_START_2.9_INCH.md for wiring)

2. Build and flash:
   $ cd examples/nrf52840_epd29
   $ ./build.sh
   $ west flash

3. View output:
   $ screen /dev/ttyACM0 115200

=== DISPLAY API ===

Initialization:
- epd_init(dev)
- epd_init_fast(dev)
- epd_init_partial(dev)

Display Operations:
- epd_clear(dev, color)
- epd_display_bw(dev, buffer)
- epd_update(dev)
- epd_update_fast(dev)
- epd_update_partial(dev)

Graphics:
- epd_paint_draw_point(dev, x, y, color)
- epd_paint_draw_line(dev, x1, y1, x2, y2, color)
- epd_paint_draw_rectangle(dev, x1, y1, x2, y2, color, filled)
- epd_paint_draw_circle(dev, cx, cy, radius, color, filled)

Text:
- epd_paint_show_string(dev, x, y, "text", size, color)
- epd_paint_show_num(dev, x, y, number, digits, size, color)

Power:
- epd_enter_deepsleepmode(dev, mode)

=== PIN CONNECTIONS (nRF52840-DK) ===

E-Paper Pin   nRF52840 Pin    Physical
-----------   ------------    -----------
VCC           3.3V            P21 pin 1
GND           GND             P21 pin 2
MOSI          P0.30           P3 pin 6
SCK           P0.29           P3 pin 5
CS            P0.31           P4 pin 1
DC            P0.04           P1 pin 2
RES           P0.03           P1 pin 1
BUSY          P0.28           P3 pin 4

=== TROUBLESHOOTING ===

Problem: Device not ready
Solution: Check power (3.3V) and all 8 wire connections

Problem: Build fails
Solution: Source Zephyr environment
   $ source ~/zephyrproject/zephyr/zephyr-env.sh

Problem: Display shows artifacts
Solution: Clear display first
   $ epd_clear(epaper_dev, EPD_COLOR_WHITE);

Problem: Out of memory
Solution: Increase heap in prj.conf
   CONFIG_HEAP_MEM_POOL_SIZE=32768
